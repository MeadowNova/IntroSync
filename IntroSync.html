Frequently Asked Questions

When will IntroSync be available?

We're currently in closed beta testing with a limited group of users. Join our waitlist to be among the first to access IntroSync when we launch publicly.

Is IntroSync only for introverts?

While IntroSync is specifically designed with introvert psychology in mind, anyone who wants to manage their social energy more effectively can benefit from our platform, including ambiverts and extroverts who experience social fatigue.

How does IntroSync protect my privacy?

We take privacy seriously. IntroSync uses end-to-end encryption for all your personal data, and we never share your information with third parties. You have complete control over what information is stored and used within the app.

Can I integrate IntroSync with my existing calendar?

Yes, IntroSync seamlessly integrates with popular calendar apps like Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar. This allows for automatic energy forecasting based on your scheduled events.
